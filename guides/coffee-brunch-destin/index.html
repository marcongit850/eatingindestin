Coffee and brunch in Destin

Need coffee before the beach? Destin doesn’t list brunch as its own meal. A cafe is the late breakfast: coffee, a bakery, or a morning plate.

Mid-Destin has the widest choice, then Miramar Beach, Sandestin, and Crystal Beach. You’ll also find cafes in Grand Boulevard, Destin Commons, and Destin Harbor.

The cafes here also serve breakfast. The breakfast guide is the wider morning list, including diners that aren’t cafes.

Open a restaurant for the hours. Weekend mornings in season draw a line at the busy spots. A coffee counter is the stop on the way to the sand. A sit-down cafe is the better plan when you want an actual breakfast. Search coffee in the directory for a few more cups that aren’t on this cafe list.
