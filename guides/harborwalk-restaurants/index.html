HarborWalk

HarborWalk restaurants

HarborWalk Village is the boardwalk at Destin Harbor. This page is that village. The Destin Harbor guide is wider: it keeps the village and the other restaurants in the harbor area.

At the boardwalk, you’ll find breakfast, lunch, dinner, a sweet stop, and a drink stop. Not every stop serves dinner, so open the restaurant if the evening is the plan.

Open a restaurant for the address if you need to stay on the village. Summer afternoons and weekends fill the boardwalk, and parking tightens up then. Go a little early if you want to sit down.

Common questions

Is HarborWalk the same as Destin Harbor?

No. HarborWalk Village is the boardwalk. The Destin Harbor guide also includes the other restaurants in the harbor area. Open Destin Harbor restaurants.

Does every HarborWalk stop serve dinner?

No. You’ll also find breakfast, sweets, and a drink stop. Show HarborWalk dinner.

Can we bring kids to HarborWalk?

Some are and some aren’t. Open the restaurant before you bring children. Show kid-friendly HarborWalk.
